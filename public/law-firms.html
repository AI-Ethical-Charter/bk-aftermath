Proposed service model

Purpose-built for the complexities of bankruptcy practice.

BK Aftermath contracts with and bills participating law firms only. Any optional client participation is administered by the participating firm under its own approved terms. Clients cannot enroll directly with BK Aftermath. All furnishing remains contingent on legal, bureau, technical and contracting approvals.

01 / Eligibility & intake

Account screening

Verify the legal creditor, signed fee agreement, chapter, petition timing, discharge treatment and approved reporting category.

02 / Data integrity

Source-ledger controls

Reconcile genuine due dates, payments, reversals, balances and changes without inventing monthly installments.

03 / Authorized processing

Metro 2 administration

Use approved coding, authorized furnisher identities, tested transmission channels and documented exceptions.

04 / Ongoing operations

Quality assurance

Apply pre- and post-submission validation, independent sign-off, account segregation and audit trails.

05 / Consumer protections

Disputes & corrections

Coordinate supported investigations, legally required updates and correction oversight with the creditor and bureaus.

06 / Partner enablement

Neutral client information

Provide your clients a neutral information page. Your firm handles any future voluntary election, eligibility and firm-established charges.

Separation of responsibilities

We don't replace your attorney-client relationship.

The actual creditor owns the attorney-fee receivable and must validate its enforceability and ledger. BK Aftermath's role is contracted administrative support. A nonlawyer administrative business is not assumed to be the creditor merely because it helps manage intake or collection records.

Designated furnishing obligations remain with the legally approved furnisher. Accurate late, closed, disputed and paid statuses cannot be excluded solely to improve credit outcomes.

Current availability:

Commercial approach

Flexible proposed contracting—with clear boundaries.

We are evaluating per-account and firm-subscription administrative pricing. Final firm fees would be established only through a selected executed schedule after legal and operational review. BK Aftermath never sets consumer participation fees or bills consumers. Each participating firm independently decides whether to charge its clients, under its own legally approved terms. Beehive Advocates is only a proposed pilot, not a universal client-pricing template.

Only BK Aftermath firm-facing fees may eventually be offered under an approved firm contract. No consumer checkout, independent sign-up, referral commission or live furnishing is available.
