How the Program Would Work ↓
Proposed client experience

A clear process before you enroll.

BK Aftermath is designed as a separate program that participating bankruptcy law firms can introduce to their clients. Your law firm would continue to own and administer your attorney-fee obligation. BK Aftermath's proposed role is to support accurate reporting of eligible accounts under an approved arrangement. Participation is not a new loan, a guaranteed credit-repair service, or a promise that positive history will appear on your credit report.

01 · Eligibility review

Confirm the underlying obligation

Your law firm and program administrator review the agreement, payment history, bankruptcy timing and applicable reporting eligibility. Some accounts cannot be reported.

02 · Disclosures and choice

Review the terms and any charges

If your referring firm offers access to the program, you would review separate program disclosures, the applicable charge, billing frequency, cancellation terms and limitations before making a decision. Your attorney-fee payment agreement and BK Aftermath's program charges must be clearly distinguished. Any proposed fee requires legal review.

03 · Verified account activity

Actual payments, not promises

Eligible data must reflect what really happened: scheduled amounts, payments, missed or late payments, adjustments, account balances and corrections as applicable.

04 · Ongoing oversight

Review, correct and dispute errors

If an authorized reporting program launches, participating entities must support accurate updates and appropriate dispute investigations. You retain your rights to question inaccurate information.

Important: A signed payment agreement is not automatically reportable. Some attorney fees incurred before a Chapter 7 bankruptcy filing may be discharged. Different issues apply to postpetition arrangements and Chapter 13 cases. Participation cannot turn a discharged debt into a valid reportable obligation.
For clients referred by a participating firm

One program. Your own law firm.

BK Aftermath is designed to work with multiple independent bankruptcy practices. Your law firm may share this informational page with you; the service is not exclusively associated with any one firm. Your attorney continues to manage your bankruptcy case and existing attorney-fee agreement.

01 · Your law firm

Introduces the option

Your firm may direct you to BK Aftermath to learn about a distinct, optional administrative service, subject to availability.

02 · BK Aftermath

Explains separate terms

Any approved enrollment would identify the services, separate price, billing terms, legal eligibility and what reporting is not guaranteed to accomplish.

03 · You

Make an informed decision

You can review the terms, ask questions and choose whether to enroll if the program becomes available. Your statutory rights remain intact.

Transparent fees

What would participation cost?

A participating law firm may refer eligible clients to BK Aftermath to consider an optional, separately billed reporting administration service. Whether consumer enrollment fees can legally be charged, who may collect them, and the amounts and timing remain subject to legal review and program approval.

Optional program fee: pricing not yet finalized

No fees are currently being collected through this website. If a paid program is offered, the agreement will explain the exact one-time and recurring fees, services included, refund and cancellation provisions, and how charges relate to any underlying attorney-fee obligation.

Enrollment and payment options will not be activated until legal review, bureau acceptance and operational readiness are complete.

A payment to BK Aftermath would not purchase a credit score, a credit-bureau listing, or favorable payment history. The permissibility of consumer charges for any credit-improvement-related service must be reviewed under applicable federal and state law.

What may appear on a report

Truthful reporting is not always positive reporting.

Where furnishing is authorized and accepted, the account history may reflect on-time payments, late or missed payments, balances, payoff and other accurate status changes, as appropriate to the type of account and bureau requirements.

There is no guarantee that a participating credit bureau will accept the account or that an account will improve a credit score. Credit scoring depends on many factors outside this program.

Consumer rights and safeguards

You keep your reporting rights.

  • Information must be based on the actual agreement and substantiated records.
  • Errors must be addressed through applicable dispute and correction procedures.
  • Participation must not require inaccurate reporting or waiver of statutory consumer rights.
  • Private information should only be collected through an approved secure process.
  • You may request information about what a proposed fee covers before agreeing to any paid service.
Questions clients ask

Frequently asked questions

Will joining automatically improve my credit score?

No. Eligibility, bureau acceptance and credit scoring are separate issues, and no score improvement is guaranteed.

Will every payment be reported as on time?

No. If furnishing is authorized, reporting must follow real payment activity. Delinquencies and other unfavorable but accurate statuses may be reportable.

Can a discharged attorney-fee debt be reported as collectible?

A discharged obligation cannot be treated as a currently enforceable debt solely because a payment schedule exists. Bankruptcy counsel must review the facts before any proposed furnishing.

Is the BK Aftermath program currently accepting participants?

Not through this website. The proposed service depends on legal review, bureau approval, secure operational processes, and finalized participant terms.

Is BK Aftermath the law firm that filed my bankruptcy?

No. BK Aftermath is developing an administrative service for participating law firms. Your attorney or law firm remains your point of contact for legal advice about your case and fee agreement.
