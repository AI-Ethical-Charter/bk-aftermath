Critical eligibility standards

Signed payment plans are not automatically reportable.

Before furnishing, the creditor and BK Aftermath must verify actual contract terms, bankruptcy filing dates, legal enforceability, ownership, and the bureau-approved account classification. Prepetition Chapter 7 obligations, valid postpetition agreements and Chapter 13 matters require distinct review. Accurate unfavorable information may also be reportable; credit score increases are never guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Questions are part of the process.

Can attorney-fee plans be reported after bankruptcy?

Not automatically. Each obligation requires review for enforceability, bankruptcy timing, account classification, and bureau acceptance. Prepetition Chapter 7 fee obligations may be dischargeable.

Will BK Aftermath guarantee an increase in my credit score?

No. No particular credit score, financing approval, or timeline can be guaranteed. Any reporting of account information must reflect real agreements and substantiated payment activity.

Who owns the attorney-fee account?

The participating law firm retains its receivable. BK Aftermath proposes to act as a contracted third-party reporting administrator, not as the creditor, a law firm, or a credit bureau.
