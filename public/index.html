Frequently asked questions

Questions are part of the process.

Can I work on my credit after bankruptcy?

Yes. Your credit profile may change over time based on many factors, including accurate reporting, payment history, balances, and the passage of time. Results vary.

Will BK Aftermath guarantee an increase in my credit score?

No. No particular credit score, financing approval, or timeline can be guaranteed. Any reporting of account information must reflect real agreements and substantiated payment activity.

Is BK Aftermath a law firm or a credit bureau?

This website is for BK Aftermath's post-bankruptcy credit recovery services. BK Aftermath is not a credit bureau. The applicable service agreement and disclosures should explain its role, program terms, and any third parties involved.
